NCBI BLAST substitution matrices

The matrices distributed by the National Center for Biotechnology Information
(https://ftp.ncbi.nih.gov/blast/matrices/) are works of a United States
government agency and are in the public domain. The BLOSUM matrices originate
from Henikoff and Henikoff, Proc. Natl. Acad. Sci. USA 89:10915 (1992).
